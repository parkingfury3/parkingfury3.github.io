About Mega Ramps: Ultimate Car Races

Get ready for a heart-racing experience as you drive powerful cars across massive ramps and breathtaking courses. Mega Ramps is not just about speed—it's about skill, precision, and the courage to tackle extreme challenges. With stunning visuals and thrilling gameplay, this game is a must-play for fans of high-stakes racing.

How to Play

Mega Ramps: Ultimate Car Races is a 3D stunt racing game where players drive across impossible ramps, perform epic stunts, and aim for the best time. Use your driving skills to navigate challenging tracks and unlock new cars and levels.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake
  • Shift: Boost
