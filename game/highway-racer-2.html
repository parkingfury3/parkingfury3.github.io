Highway Racer 2

★★★★★ 46 votes

Highway Racer 2 delivers intense highway driving through busy rush-hour traffic with oncoming vehicles, high-speed near-misses, and dynamic weather.

About Highway Racer 2

Highway Racer 2 features realistic cockpit views, multiple game modes (One-Way, Two-Way, Time Attack, Speed Bomb), and extensive car tuning options. Push your speedometer past limits for big near-miss cash bonuses.

How to Play

Weave through dense highway lanes, scrape past vehicles for near-miss multipliers, and brake quickly when traffic grinds to a halt.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
