Drift Escape

★★★★★ 45 votes

Drift Escape is a high-stakes police chase survival game where sharp drifting maneuvers are your only hope of evading relentless cop cruisers.

About Drift Escape

Drift Escape challenges your reaction speed in an open arena swarming with police cars, SWAT vans, and armored vehicles. Lure pursuers into head-on collisions with each other through precision drift turns.

How to Play

Tap left and right to drift your getaway car in tight circles, dodge incoming cruisers, and trick cop cars into crashing into each other.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
