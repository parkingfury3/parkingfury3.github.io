3D Moto Simulator

Experience the thrill of riding powerful motorcycles in an open-world setting with 3D Moto Simulator. This game offers an exciting and immersive experience, allowing players to explore vast environments, perform daring stunts, and push their bikes to the limit.

About 3D Moto Simulator

3D Moto Simulator is all about freedom and speed. With realistic physics and detailed graphics, it provides a unique blend of relaxation and adrenaline. Whether you're cruising through city streets, racing at full speed, or just enjoying the scenery, this game has something for every motorcycle enthusiast.

How to Play

3D Moto Simulator is a free-roam game where you control a motorcycle in various environments using the keyboard.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake
