Moto Road Rash 3D 2 takes you on a heart-pounding journey through highways, city streets, and open roads. Speed past traffic, perform daring maneuvers, and prove your skills in this high-octane motorcycle racing game.

About Moto Road Rash 3D 2

This sequel to the popular Moto Road Rash series offers improved graphics, smoother controls, and even more challenging races. Dodge traffic, compete against AI riders, and unlock new motorcycles as you rise through the ranks. With its realistic physics and immersive gameplay, this game delivers a thrilling ride for motorcycle enthusiasts.

How to Play

Moto Road Rash 3D 2 is a 3D motorcycle racing game that tests your reflexes and racing instincts. Navigate through intense traffic, complete missions, and earn rewards to upgrade your bike and customize your experience.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Shift: Nitro Boost
