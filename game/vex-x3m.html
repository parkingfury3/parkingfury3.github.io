VEX X3M

★★★★★ 86 votes

Stick figures are often perplexed by the extreme scenarios they find themselves in, and the Vex team is no different in VEX X3M. This group of stick figures is out to prove they are the most extreme by riding motorcycles through the wildest tracks filled with dangerous jumps and obstacles. Can they make it to the finish line without crashing?

About VEX X3M

VEX X3M is a thrilling 2D arcade game where you control a stick figure riding a motorcycle through increasingly challenging tracks. The game combines skill and precision as you navigate complex obstacles, perform stunts, and race against time. While crashes are inevitable, the key to success is mastering the controls and overcoming each difficult track.

How to Play

Guide your stick figure on a motorcycle through a series of dangerous and twisted tracks. Avoid obstacles, perform stunts, and race to the finish line. Be careful not to crash as you aim to complete each level in the fastest time possible.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Decelerate/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Tilt Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Tilt Right
