G-Switch

★★★★★ 48 votes

G-Switch is an adrenaline-fueled gravity-inversion runner where one touch flips your character between running on the floor and sprinting on the ceiling.

About G-Switch

G-Switch challenges your reflex timing across futuristic obstacle platforms. Invert gravity mid-stride, leap over fatal gaps, and compete in crazy multi-player races on the same keyboard.

How to Play

Press Space or Left Click at the exact right millisecond to flip gravity, avoid falling into the void, and outrun relentless chase hazards.

Controls

  • Mouse Click / Space: Action / Jump / Gravity Switch
  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Move / Steer
