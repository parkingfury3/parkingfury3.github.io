Burnin' Rubber 5 XS is a 3D vehicular combat and demolition racing game equipped with heavy machine guns, nitro boosters, and explosive rockets.

About Burnin' Rubber 5 XS

Burnin' Rubber 5 XS combines extreme racing speeds with explosive demolition battles. Outfit muscle cars and armored trucks with destructive weaponry and eliminate rivals in fierce death-races.

How to Play

Race at breakneck speeds while firing rockets and bullets at rival cars. Collect money, complete bounty contracts, and upgrade your war machine.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
