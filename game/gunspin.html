Gunspin

★★★★★ 44 votes

Gunspin is an addictive physics launch game where weapon recoil propels your gun across desert landscapes to break distance records.

About Gunspin

Gunspin challenges you to fire bullets at optimal trajectory angles to keep your gun flying. Upgrade bullet counts, recoil power, bouncing velocity, and unlock heavy revolvers, shotguns, and laser rifles.

How to Play

Click to shoot bullets when your gun barrel is angled backward, using recoil bursts to propel the firearm forward across terrain markers.

Controls

  • Mouse Click / Space: Action / Jump / Gravity Switch
  • Arrow Keys / WASD: Move / Steer
