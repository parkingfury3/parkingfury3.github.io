★★★★★ 52 votes

Fireboy and Watergirl 4: Crystal Temple tests your spatial problem solving with magical crystal teleporters that transport heroes across rooms.

About Fireboy and Watergirl 4: Crystal Temple

Fireboy and Watergirl 4: Crystal Temple features color-coded crystal portals that alter physical orientation and momentum. Plan each step carefully to avoid getting trapped.

How to Play

Step through matching crystal teleporters, time switch activations, collect precious gems, and exit the chamber without touching lethal hazards.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Move Fireboy (Lava immune)
  • A, W, D: Move Watergirl (Water immune)
  • Space / Lever: Activate elevators and switches
