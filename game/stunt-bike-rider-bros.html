Stunt Bike Rider Bros

★★★★☆ 38 votes

Get ready for an adrenaline-fueled ride in Stunt Bike Rider Bros! This exciting game challenges you to perform daring stunts and navigate challenging tracks with your bike. The more tricks you pull off, the higher your score, but be careful not to crash as you race through each level.

About Stunt Bike Rider Bros

Stunt Bike Rider Bros is all about mastering the art of stunts and high-speed bike riding. With each level, you’ll face increasingly difficult tracks filled with jumps, loops, and obstacles. The game rewards precision, skill, and timing, while offering a fun and exciting experience for stunt enthusiasts.

How to Play

Use your bike to perform stunts, avoid obstacles, and reach the finish line in the fastest time possible. The key to success is mastering the controls and landing your tricks without crashing.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Tilt Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Tilt Right
  • Spacebar: Perform Stunt
