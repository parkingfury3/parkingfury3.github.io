2 Player Dark Racing delivers high-octane racing action across atmospheric midnight race tracks. Challenge a friend in head-to-head split-screen mode or test your speed against cunning AI drivers.

About 2 Player Dark Racing

2 Player Dark Racing immerses players in thrilling neon-lit circuits and dangerous turns. With responsive physics, customizable supercars, and split-screen multiplayer mode, it delivers competitive driving excitement for one or two players.

How to Play

Navigate twisting night-time racecourses, hit speed boosts, drift around sharp bends, and cross the finish line ahead of your rival.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
