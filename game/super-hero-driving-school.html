Super Hero Driving School

★★★★☆ 45 votes

Put your super driving skills to the test in Super Hero Driving School! As a superhero, you’ll need to master the art of high-speed driving, navigate through challenging obstacle courses, and complete driving missions with precision to graduate from the superhero academy.

About Super Hero Driving School

Super Hero Driving School offers a unique and fun twist on traditional driving games. As a superhero, you’ll be learning how to drive fast and efficiently in extreme environments. With powerful vehicles, dangerous stunts, and epic missions, this game provides an exciting combination of action and skill development.

How to Play

Complete various driving challenges and missions while avoiding obstacles, collecting items, and mastering your superhero driving skills. Use your super abilities to help you navigate tough courses and become the best driver in the academy!

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Spacebar: Use Super Ability
