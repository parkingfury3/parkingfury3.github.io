Parking Fury 3D: Night Thief

★★★★☆ 38 votes

Embrace the shadows in Parking Fury 3D: Night Thief! As night falls, take on the role of a daring driver tasked with stealing cars and delivering them to secret locations. Navigate the dark streets, avoid law enforcement, and show off your stealth and parking skills in this thrilling night-time adventure.

About Parking Fury 3D: Night Thief

Parking Fury 3D: Night Thief combines stealth and precision driving into a captivating gameplay experience. Set in a moody, open-world environment, you'll need to complete high-stakes missions while evading police. The combination of immersive 3D graphics, challenging objectives, and night-time atmosphere makes this a standout game for fans of action and driving.

How to Play

Drive through the city under cover of darkness, steal designated cars, and deliver them to the drop-off points. Avoid police patrols and obstacles to complete your mission without getting caught!

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Shift: Activate Nitro
