Bomb It 6 features intense arcade maze action with enhanced graphics, new character classes, and thrilling mini-game challenges.

About Bomb It 6

Bomb It 6 raises the stakes with diverse game modes such as Zone Capture, Survival, and Treasure Hunt. Utilize freeze rays, rocket launchers, and mega bombs to blast your way to victory.

How to Play

Plant bombs strategically to open passages and trap enemy characters, all while staying clear of incoming blast radiuses.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Move your bomber character
  • Spacebar: Place bomb
  • Power-ups: Collect speed boosts, shields, and super bombs
