Jump Into The Plane

★★★★★ 43 votes

Jump Into The Plane is a high-flying stunt arcade game where you launch vehicles from massive ramps straight into the cargo hold of flying airplanes!

About Jump Into The Plane

Jump Into The Plane challenges players to gauge slingshot speed, nitro boosts, and mid-air trajectory. Smash through sky barriers, glide through clouds, and land squarely inside the transport aircraft.

How to Play

Launch down the booster ramp, fire your jet thrusters in mid-air, steer your trajectory, and land inside the open airplane hatch.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
