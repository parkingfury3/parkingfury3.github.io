Snail Bob 8: Island Story shipwrecks Bob onto a mysterious tropical island populated by clever island tribes and exotic wildlife.

About Snail Bob 8: Island Story

Snail Bob 8: Island Story challenges players with volcanic vents, tribal traps, totem puzzles, and rope bridges across uncharted island territory.

How to Play

Disarm tribal traps, activate ancient totem switches, dodge dangerous critters, and lead Bob safely to his rescue boat.

Controls

  • Mouse Click: Start / Stop Snail Bob
  • 1 / Space: Turn Snail Bob around
  • 2: Speed up Bob's crawl speed
  • Mouse Click: Activate switches, buttons & platforms
