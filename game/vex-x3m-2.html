Vex X3M 2

★★★★★ 53 votes

Vex X3M 2 combines hardcore motocross racing with lethal Vex-style obstacle courses for an explosive, stunt-packed motorcycle thrill ride.

About Vex X3M 2

Vex X3M 2 challenges dirt bike riders to flip over giant buzzsaws, launch off TNT-loaded ramps, and survive deathtrap motocross circuits across 30+ intense stages.

How to Play

Lean forward and backward to balance your motorcycle in mid-air, hit throttle to power up vertical loops, and execute flips for time bonuses.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
