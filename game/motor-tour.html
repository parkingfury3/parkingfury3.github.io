Motor Tour

★★★★★ 48 votes

Motor Tour takes you on an exhilarating journey through endless highways. Jump on your motorcycle and experience stunning visuals, smooth controls, and high-speed challenges. Whether it's dodging traffic or racing against the clock, Motor Tour promises non-stop action.

About Motor Tour

This game is a thrilling blend of speed and skill, putting players in control of powerful motorcycles on dynamic roads. With multiple game modes, including time trials and traffic dodging, Motor Tour offers endless entertainment for motorcycle enthusiasts. Customize your bike, compete with other players, and explore diverse landscapes as you race to the finish line.

How to Play

Motor Tour is a 3D motorcycle racing game where players navigate busy highways, dodge traffic, and complete various objectives. Use your driving skills to earn rewards and unlock new bikes and tracks.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Use Nitro Boost
