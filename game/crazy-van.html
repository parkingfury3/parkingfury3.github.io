Crazy Van

★★★★☆ 52 votes

Buckle up for a wild ride in Crazy Van! Take control of a quirky van as you navigate chaotic roads, dodge obstacles, and deliver cargo in the most unconventional ways. Get ready for unpredictable fun!

About Crazy Van

Crazy Van is a hilarious driving game where physics and creativity take the wheel. Players must overcome wild terrain, unexpected hazards, and tight deadlines to complete their deliveries. With wacky upgrades and colorful graphics, every drive is a new adventure.

How to Play

Crazy Van is a 2D driving game where you balance speed, precision, and chaos to complete your missions. Keep your cargo safe and reach your destination without flipping your van!

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Tilt Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Tilt Right
  • Space: Jump over obstacles
