Night City Racing

★★★★★ 49 votes

Night City Racing invites you to burn rubber on neon-lit city streets in high-end hypercars across racing, challenge, and free drive modes.

About Night City Racing

Night City Racing features hyper-realistic 3D graphics, nitro acceleration, detailed vehicle customization, and split-screen 2-player racing through sprawling nocturnal skylines.

How to Play

Race against top street racers, activate nitro boosts on open straights, drift through corners, and upgrade engine parts to reach top speeds.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
