ATV Ultimate Offroad

★★★★★ 43 votes

ATV Ultimate Offroad puts you behind the handlebars of extreme quad bikes traversing rugged terrains, steep rocky hills, and muddy mountain trails.

About ATV Ultimate Offroad

ATV Ultimate Offroad offers realistic quad bike handling across diverse offroad environments. Tackle thrilling free drive modes, participate in championship races, and unlock high-powered ATVs equipped with heavy-duty suspension.

How to Play

Control your four-wheeler over harsh terrain, balance mid-air over giant jumps, and reach each checkpoint before time runs out.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
