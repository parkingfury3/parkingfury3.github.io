Fireboy and Watergirl 6: Fairy Tales

★★★★★ 54 votes

Fireboy and Watergirl 6: Fairy Tales introduces glowing elemental fairies that can be directed to activate far-off energy switches and doors.

About Fireboy and Watergirl 6: Fairy Tales

Fireboy and Watergirl 6: Fairy Tales enriches classic cooperative gameplay with fairy helpers. Drag and guide magical fairies to energize laser relays and unlock difficult temple passages.

How to Play

Use keyboard keys to navigate Fireboy and Watergirl, and use your mouse to command glowing fairies to open high-altitude switches.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Move Fireboy (Lava immune)
  • A, W, D: Move Watergirl (Water immune)
  • Space / Lever: Activate elevators and switches
