Real Cars: Epic Stunts

★★★★☆ 58 votes

Push the limits of driving in Real Cars: Epic Stunts! This adrenaline-filled game lets you take control of powerful cars as you perform incredible stunts, navigate treacherous tracks, and conquer daring challenges. Can you handle the thrill?

About Real Cars: Epic Stunts

Real Cars: Epic Stunts combines realistic car physics with thrilling stunt-based gameplay. Explore massive open-world environments filled with ramps, loops, and obstacles designed to test your driving skills. Whether you're chasing high scores or just having fun with crazy tricks, this game delivers non-stop excitement.

How to Play

Take control of your car and complete stunt challenges by launching off ramps, dodging obstacles, and mastering sharp turns. Use precision and speed to achieve the best results and unlock new vehicles and tracks.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Shift: Activate Nitro
  • Spacebar: Handbrake
