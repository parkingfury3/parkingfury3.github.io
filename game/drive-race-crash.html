Experience the chaotic thrill of "Drive Race Crash," a game where speed meets destruction. Race through challenging tracks, push your car to the limit, and enjoy the exhilarating crashes along the way.

About Drive Race Crash

"Drive Race Crash" combines high-speed racing with spectacular collisions. Whether you aim to finish first or cause maximum destruction, every race is an adrenaline-filled adventure. With unpredictable tracks and intense competition, this game promises endless excitement.

How to Play

Drive Race Crash is a 3D arcade-style racing game where the goal is to either win the race or enjoy the chaos of crashing. Navigate through tricky obstacles, outrun opponents, and embrace the unpredictability of each track.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake
