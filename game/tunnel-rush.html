Tunnel Rush

★★★★★ 55 votes

Tunnel Rush is a high-speed 3D kaleidoscopic reflex game where you hurtle through revolving neon tunnels avoiding rapidly shifting barriers.

About Tunnel Rush

Tunnel Rush tests your lightning-fast reaction skills in an intense, pulsating futuristic tunnel. Dodge rotating triangles, swinging blocks, and color-shifting barriers at supersonic speeds.

How to Play

Use Left and Right Arrow keys or A and D to rotate around the circular tunnel walls and slip through openings in advancing obstacles.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
