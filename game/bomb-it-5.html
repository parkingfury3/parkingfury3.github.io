About Bomb It 5

Bomb It 5 offers expanded vehicles, supercharged weapons, and colorful arenas. Play solo against smart bots or grab a buddy for chaotic 2-player local matches.

How to Play

Drop bombs wisely, blast open paths to collect mystery power-ups, and outmaneuver all opposing bots to be the last survivor standing.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Move your bomber character
  • Spacebar: Place bomb
  • Power-ups: Collect speed boosts, shields, and super bombs
