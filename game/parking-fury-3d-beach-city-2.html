Parking Fury 3D: Beach City 2

Experience the seaside vibes and challenging parking tasks in Parking Fury 3D: Beach City 2! This game tests your driving precision as you explore the vibrant streets of a beachside city, completing parking missions while avoiding traffic and obstacles.

About Parking Fury 3D: Beach City 2

Parking Fury 3D: Beach City 2 brings you back to the sunny beach city for another round of intense parking challenges. With stunning 3D graphics and a lively environment, this game offers a mix of skillful driving, strategic parking, and exploration. Whether you’re navigating tight spaces or cruising through scenic streets, the fun never stops!

How to Play

Drive around the beach city and complete parking objectives in the designated spots. Avoid collisions with other vehicles and obstacles while aiming for precision. The better you park, the higher your score!

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Shift: Activate Nitro
  • Spacebar: Handbrake
