Mr Racer: Car Racing

★★★★★ 50 votes

Get ready to experience the thrill of high-speed racing in "Mr Racer: Car Racing." Take control of sleek, high-performance cars and race through dynamic tracks, dodging traffic and aiming for the top spot. Can you prove you're the ultimate racer?

About Mr Racer: Car Racing

Mr Racer: Car Racing offers an adrenaline-packed racing experience where players compete against time and traffic in a fast-paced environment. Featuring stunning graphics, a variety of cars, and challenging tracks, this game delivers the excitement and intensity every racing enthusiast craves.

How to Play

Mr Racer: Car Racing is a 3D racing game that lets players race against AI opponents or challenge themselves in time trials. Use your driving skills to navigate through traffic, avoid collisions, and achieve the fastest time.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Shift: Nitro Boost
  • Space: Handbrake
