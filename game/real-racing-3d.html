About Real Racing 3D

Real Racing 3D brings a new level of realism to mobile racing games. With a wide selection of cars, tracks, and modes, this game offers an immersive racing experience. Customize your car, master each course, and dominate the competition in a variety of fast-paced challenges. Its realistic visuals and smooth gameplay will make you feel like you're truly on the racetrack.

How to Play

Drive your car on various race tracks, aiming to finish ahead of your opponents. Use your skills to navigate tight corners, manage your speed, and outsmart the competition. Upgrade your car to improve its performance and rise through the ranks.

Controls

  • W/Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S/Down Arrow: Brake/Reverse
  • A/Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D/Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Spacebar: Nitro Boost
