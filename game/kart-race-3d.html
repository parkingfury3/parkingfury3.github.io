Kart Race 3D

★★★★★ 45 votes

Kart Race 3D is a lively go-kart racing game filled with colorful tracks, tight hairpin turns, bouncy speed pads, and fun power-ups.

About Kart Race 3D

Kart Race 3D delivers arcade kart racing excitement for all ages. Drift around colorful amusement park circuits, grab turbo boosts, and out-maneuver competing racers to claim first place on the podium.

How to Play

Steer smoothly into corners, drift across apex lines for speed boosts, and collect turbo stars to blast past the competition.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
