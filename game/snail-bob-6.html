Snail Bob 6: Winter Story

★★★★★ 51 votes

Snail Bob 6: Winter Story is a cozy holiday puzzle quest where Bob sets out into a snowy winter wonderland to rescue Santa from the evil green Grinch.

About Snail Bob 6: Winter Story

Snail Bob 6: Winter Story features slippery ice rinks, snow catapults, falling icicles, and festive holiday cheer. Help Bob save Christmas!

How to Play

Navigate frozen platforms, operate holiday gadgetry, build snow bridges, and solve seasonal physics puzzles.

Controls

  • Mouse Click: Start / Stop Snail Bob
  • 1 / Space: Turn Snail Bob around
  • 2: Speed up Bob's crawl speed
  • Mouse Click: Activate switches, buttons & platforms
