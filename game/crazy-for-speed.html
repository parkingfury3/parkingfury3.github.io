Crazy for Speed

★★★★★ 42 votes

Crazy for Speed puts you in the driver's seat of exotic sports cars racing through bustling metropolitan traffic and scenic coastal highways.

About Crazy for Speed

Crazy for Speed features realistic engine acoustics, dynamic day-and-night cycles, and thrilling time trials against the clock and rival street racers.

How to Play

Weave through dense highway traffic at over 200 mph, collect nitro powerups, and cross the finish line to unlock next-generation hypercars.

Controls

  • W / Up Arrow: Accelerate
  • S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse
  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Space: Handbrake / Drift
